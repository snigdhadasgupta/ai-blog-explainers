ClaudeAgents

The Anatomy of Effective Commerce Agents

Anthropic's engineering deep-dive on commerce agents: use one model in an agent loop with skills, not a subagent swarm, because handoffs lose state and cost tokens and seconds. The wins are in latency (design for 90–99% prompt-cache hit rates), streaming for perceived speed, and enforcing every irreversible action in the harness so the model only ever stages a change a person or policy applies.
