BoxModel Launch

Claude Haiku 5.5: More Accurate, Half the Latency

Box ran Claude Haiku 5.5 through its Complex Work Eval — agents that locate documents and reason across spreadsheets, PDFs and slides against weighted rubrics. Haiku 5.5 scored 60% overall versus 49% for Haiku 4.5 (+11 points), used about 27% fewer tokens, and produced deliverables in roughly half the time. The biggest gains were in arithmetic-heavy work — report drafting (+19) and data analysis (+10) — with financial services rising from 48% to 71%.

Microsoft SecurityAI Governance

3 Lessons from Frontier AI Vulnerability Research

Microsoft’s FORGE Lab used AI agents for vulnerability research across Windows and open source, producing 140 Windows CVEs from May to September 2026 and 155 internally validated reports across 23 open-source projects. The takeaway in three lessons: optimize for reproducible findings rather than scan volume, spend reasoning where it removes a specific uncertainty, and fold validation and patching into the discovery loop. As AI finds bugs faster than teams can fix them, the bottleneck shifts from discovery to triage, validation and remediation.

Microsoft SecurityAI Governance

CISO Perspectives: Vulnerability Risk in the Age of AI

Two Microsoft security leaders make the CISO case that in an AI-accelerated world, remediation — not scanning — is the bottleneck: AI speeds weakness-finding and exploit development alike, and on-premises Patch Tuesday volumes are rising, with September 2026 setting a record near 1,000 vulnerabilities. Their playbook: resource remediation as a planned budget line, shorten patch timing for critical systems to as little as 24 hours, adopt AI scanning and remediation harnesses now, and strengthen defense in depth on the assumption that not every vulnerability will be patched in time.

BoxCase Study

Bolt Turns Weeks of Compliance Work into Minutes

Bolt, the urban-mobility platform operating in 50+ countries with 4,000+ employees and 170,000+ employee documents in Box, faced a compliance backlog that would have taken about two years to audit by hand. It built an HR workflow combining Box Automate (consistent renaming, folder creation and routing), Box Extract (metadata tagging) and Box Apps (a queryable dashboard), pulling documents from both Box and Workday into one content layer. Compliance questions that once took weeks or months — such as how many Belgian employees have a remote-work clause — are now answered in minutes, with staff shifting from manual checks to reviewing AI output.

A Practical Guide to Building with GPT-6

OpenAI's practical guide to GPT-6 focuses on production discipline: match Astra (hardest reasoning), Sol (near-Astra at a fifth the cost) or Luna (fast everyday) to the workload, cache input tokens for up to 95% savings, specify outcomes rather than rigid instructions, and use mid-turn steering and async tool calling to keep long-running agents productive and supervised.
