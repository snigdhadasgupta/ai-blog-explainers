Gemini 4 Argon Pushes the Frontier of Enterprise Work

Managing Open-Weight Model Risk

Microsoft and NVIDIA argue weight-openness isn't a risk rating — an open-weight model's risk comes from its use case, deployment, and safeguards. Their new joint whitepaper gives a shared five-category taxonomy (cybersecurity, data protection, content, human-AI interaction, bias) so teams speak one language, maps each risk to evaluations (Microsoft Foundry adds groundedness, safety scoring, and automated red teaming), and ties every risk to a working control and an accountable owner — revisited as models and deployments change.

BoxAI Governance

Governing Third-Party AI Agents

AI that advises became AI that acts, so Box argues a clean vendor review tells you nothing about what a single agent can actually do inside your systems. Govern each agent by its access and authority with two separate levers: least privilege (what it can see) and least agency (what it can decide). Enforce boundaries while the agent acts via a five-step cycle, and don't let trust auto-pass down multi-agent delegation chains.

What to Look For in AI Enterprise Search

Most employees still can't find the file they need, and bolting a chatbot onto a broken index doesn't fix it. Box's guide separates AI enterprise search from keyword search: intent-based, permission-aware retrieval that grounds answers in source documents you can verify. The practical core is a five-capability buying checklist — semantic retrieval, agentic refinement, permission-aware relevance, metadata-driven accuracy, and unified access across silos — plus a four-phase rollout and the metrics to track. Cited stats are third-party.

BoxEnterprise AI

Getting Unstructured Data AI-Ready

Agents reason from the messy 80% of enterprise information — documents, scans, contracts, chat. Box's State of AI Report 2026 finds 96% of organizations say agents need company content, yet 49% have already had an AI data-exposure incident and only 34% have formal access rules. The fix is governance plus quality: index and enrich content where it lives, exclude sensitive or stale files before ingestion, and deliver curated datasets instead of file dumps. Box customers have classified more than 7 billion files.
