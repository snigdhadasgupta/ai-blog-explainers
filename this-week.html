Gemini 4 Argon Pushes the Frontier of Enterprise Work

Box ran Google’s new Gemini 4 Argon through its Complex Work Eval — agents handed real bundles of spreadsheets, PDFs, slides and images and graded on the finished deliverable. It scored 85% overall versus 71% for Gemini 3.1 Pro (+14 points), with the biggest gains in data analysis (+23) and report drafting (+15). Regulated domains moved most: life sciences 61→88, healthcare 57→87 and public sector 76→94. The model is “coming soon” to Box AI; these are Box’s own benchmark numbers, so validate on your own workloads.

BoxModel Launch

Claude Haiku 5.5: More Accurate, Half the Latency

Box ran Claude Haiku 5.5 through its Complex Work Eval — agents that locate documents and reason across spreadsheets, PDFs and slides against weighted rubrics. Haiku 5.5 scored 60% overall versus 49% for Haiku 4.5 (+11 points), used about 27% fewer tokens, and produced deliverables in roughly half the time. The biggest gains were in arithmetic-heavy work — report drafting (+19) and data analysis (+10) — with financial services rising from 48% to 71%.

3 Lessons from Frontier AI Vulnerability Research

Microsoft’s FORGE Lab used AI agents for vulnerability research across Windows and open source, producing 140 Windows CVEs from May to September 2026 and 155 internally validated reports across 23 open-source projects. The takeaway in three lessons: optimize for reproducible findings rather than scan volume, spend reasoning where it removes a specific uncertainty, and fold validation and patching into the discovery loop. As AI finds bugs faster than teams can fix them, the bottleneck shifts from discovery to triage, validation and remediation.

Microsoft 365Agents

CRM in the Flow of Work, Agents in the Process

Microsoft is inverting the CRM model — customer context and actions come to people inside Copilot and Teams instead of the other way around. Thirty pre-built Dynamics 365 skills across Sales, Service, and Customer Insights land in Copilot's Cowork, Autopilot, and Code experiences, powered by Dynamics 365 MCP servers. Autonomous agents run whole processes: a Service Agent handles cases end to end across Outlook, Teams, and Office, and a Sales Development Agent builds and qualifies pipeline from its own Exchange inbox (consuming Copilot credits from Nov 16). Dynamics 365 Activate maps a lower-risk path off legacy CRM, and everything is governed through Agent 365.
