T. Rowe Price Puts Claude at the Core of Investing

The idea in five lines

  • Started at the front office — portfolio managers and analysts use Claude Cowork to synthesize research, not just automate back-office tasks.
  • Three primary use cases — research synthesis, multi-step knowledge-intensive processes, and developers building their own tools with Claude Code.
  • Coverage, then depth — the stated goal is to let investment professionals cover more ground and go deeper on what matters — freeing analysts for judgment and debate.
  • Governance is structural — AI leadership sits inside Investments and Global Distribution, and T. Rowe Price Labs evaluates and scales applications.
  • The business owns the AI — every application is owned by its business unit under responsible-use standards, keeping human judgment at the center of client outcomes.
So what for you

If you are scoping an AI rollout, resist the reflex to pilot only in the back office. Pick one high-value decision workflow your experts actually run, put AI in as a research accelerant, and name a business owner for it — the deployments that keep the expert in the loop are the ones that scale past the pilot.

Why it’s notable

AI where the decisions get made

When an asset manager deploys AI, the safe move is the back office — reconciliation, reporting, IT. T. Rowe Price instead pointed Claude at the investment process itself: professionals use Cowork to synthesize complex information so analysts can spend their time on deeper analysis and debate.

“Most enterprise AI deployments start with the back office. T. Rowe Price started with the people who pick the securities.” — Peter Nolan, Anthropic

How the work splits

Three ways Claude shows up

Research

Synthesis, not answers

Investment professionals use Cowork to pull together complex information across sources, so human analysts can go deeper on what actually moves a decision.

Operations

Multi-step processes

Knowledge-intensive workflows across investing and operations teams are handled end-to-end rather than task-by-task.

Engineering

Build-your-own tools

Developers on investment and operations teams use Claude Code to build the tools their own workflows need.
