The theme

Creation moves inside the app

Google’s July Workspace drop is unified by one idea: generation happening in place, inside the tool you’re already using. Instead of bouncing to a separate AI surface, Gemini now builds decks in Slides, turns them into scripted avatar videos in Vids, acts on comments and creates visuals in Docs, and puts Meet within hands-free reach in the car.

The point isn’t a new AI destination — it’s the creation step collapsing into the surface where the work already lives.
