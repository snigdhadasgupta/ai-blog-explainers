Box for ServiceNow, Natively

Your process lives in ServiceNow; your documents live in Box — and until now, working a ticket meant bouncing between the two. The new native, ServiceNow-certified Box integration brings the content into the record itself, with governance intact.

The idea in five lines

  • Process and content, reunited — ServiceNow runs the workflow, Box holds the files, and this certified integration finally puts the files inside the record.
  • No more context-switching — a Box panel on each record lets people preview, upload, and share content without ever leaving the ticket, case, or request.
  • A folder per record, automatically — every incident, case, or request gets its own Box folder across ITSM, HR, CSM, CRM, or custom modules.
  • Governance travels with the file — content keeps Box version control, classification, legal hold, and retention, with Box roles mapped to ServiceNow users.
  • No middleware to maintain — a certified scoped app from the ServiceNow Store that keeps working as both platforms update, at no extra charge on Box Business plans and up.
So what for you

If your teams swivel between ServiceNow and a content tool to work a ticket or case, this removes the hop — but the quieter win is that files stay governed in Box instead of being copied into ServiceNow. Worth checking whether your current workflow is silently duplicating sensitive documents across systems.

The gap it closes

Two systems that never quite met

Earlier integrations could shuttle Box files through ServiceNow workflows behind the scenes — but the person actually working a ticket, case, or contract had no native way to open, preview, or share the relevant content without leaving ServiceNow and switching to Box. The new integration closes that gap by keeping each system doing what it does best.

What you get

The content, right on the record

A Box panel sits on the ServiceNow record: navigate folders, preview files, upload, and share — in the same place you manage the work.

Every record gets its own Box folder, created automatically, whether it lives in ITSM, HR service delivery, customer service management, CRM, or a custom module. Because the files live in Box and are surfaced in ServiceNow rather than duplicated, there’s a single copy under a single set of controls.

Governance that comes along for the ride

Content keeps the Box policies it already has — version control, classification, legal hold, and retention — and Box roles can be assigned to ServiceNow users so people see only what they’re authorized to access. Box lists the following compliance coverage (its own claims):

How it’s built

A certified app, not a custom connector

The integration is a certified scoped application on the ServiceNow Store — so there’s no custom-coded connector and no third-party middleware to build or babysit, and Box says it keeps working as either platform ships updates. Setup is a matter of connecting a Box account and configuring access.

What to do with this

If you already run both Box and ServiceNow, pick one high-friction workflow — incident evidence, HR onboarding, or contract review — and pilot the native panel there. Confirm the Box-role-to-ServiceNow-user mapping matches your access policy, and check whether you can retire any file copies ServiceNow was holding.
