August Workspace Drops

This month’s Google Workspace update targets the busywork around meetings and repeatable tasks: slide screenshots inside Meet notes, auto-organized meeting artifacts, quiz generation in Forms, reusable Gemini skills, and new admin guardrails for no-code agents.

The idea in five lines

  • Richer meeting notes — Meet’s “Take notes for me” now adds slide screenshots, so summaries capture both the what and the visual why.
  • No more file hunting — Meet auto-files notes, transcripts, and recordings into one searchable “Google Meet” Drive folder, with shortcuts for attendees.
  • Quizzes from a prompt — Forms’ “Help me create” generates a full quiz, answers and settings included, from a prompt plus referenced Drive files.
  • Reusable Workspace skills — package your team’s rules and templates into a Gemini skill, invoked via @mention across Gmail, Docs, Slides, Drive, and Chat.
  • Admin guardrails for agents — new Studio controls let admins suspend agents or OAuth scopes and apply Gemini and Studio DLP to no-code automations.
So what for you

If your team runs on meetings, turn on Meet’s auto-organized artifacts to kill the post-meeting file hunt — then pilot a single Workspace skill for one recurring formatting task (status updates or proposals) and see how much a reusable prompt actually saves before rolling it out widely.

The theme

Three fronts: meetings, content creation, and reuse

The August drop is less about flashy new surfaces and more about removing friction from things people already do every day — sitting in meetings, building training material, and repeating the same formatting chores. It clusters into three areas, with a fourth track of admin controls to keep the new agentic capabilities governed.

Meetings & content

Killing the two biggest meeting chores

Google Meet

Context, not just bullets

“Take notes for me” now captures screenshots of presented slides alongside key takeaways, so a summary carries the ‘what’ and the ‘why’ — no more decoding vague references like “as you can see on this slide.”

Google Meet

One folder, no follow-ups

Meet auto-organizes notes, transcripts, and recordings into a parent “Google Meet” folder in the host’s Drive, and gives attendees shortcuts — turning scattered files into a persistent, searchable meeting hub.

Google Forms

Quizzes on demand

Describe the quiz you want and reference Docs, Slides, or PDFs from Drive; Gemini generates a complete quiz — questions, settings, and correct answers — ready to preview, adjust, and share for onboarding or training.

Rollout

Available now

These updates are live for Google Workspace customers, arriving inside the tools teams already use rather than as a separate destination.

Reuse & governance

Skills scale processes; Studio controls keep them safe

The most durable additions are about turning one-off prompting into repeatable process. With skills in Workspace Studio, you build and share a reusable prompt that guides Gemini to follow your team’s rules, templates, and reference files — a specialized AI teammate that lives in the side panel and is invoked via @mention across Gmail, Docs, Slides, Drive, and Chat. Common uses: enforcing brand tone and voice, formatting proposals and contracts to spec, and keeping status updates in a standard format.

Because those skills and no-code flows can act on real data, Google is expanding enterprise security controls in Studio Flows so admins can adopt agentic automation with confidence:

Access

Agent access management

Admins can view and suspend all agents — or targeted OAuth scopes for individual flows, such as revoking Drive access — from an agent access management dashboard in the console.

Gemini DLP

Restrict what Gemini reads

Gemini data loss prevention limits Gemini’s access to Drive data based on content conditions and labels, with more services coming.

Studio DLP

Gate flow execution

Studio DLP can block or force end-user review of a Studio Flow based on the sourced data, the data it uses, and the visibility of its output.

Where to start this week

Switch on Meet’s auto-organized artifacts so meeting notes stop scattering across Drive, then stand up one Workspace skill for your team’s most repetitive formatting task. If you administer the tenant, review the new Studio agent controls and DLP before broadly enabling no-code automations.
