Introducing Claude Duet

Claude Duet is a new app where Claude works as a proactive agent — it brings you what needs attention each morning, keeps working while you step away, and checks with you before it acts. A research preview, by waitlist, for US Pro and Max subscribers.

The idea in five lines

  • A proactive agent app — Duet comes to you with what needs attention, completes tasks you assign, and keeps working even when you step away.
  • Built on Opus 5.5 — it is designed to decide what needs you, what can wait, and when to check in — and runs on its own virtual machine with a browser, so your laptop can be off.
  • A morning brief — each day it sends a short brief on the most important items across your connected apps, and checks those apps every few hours, not constantly, to conserve your plan limits.
  • It asks before it acts — before sending a message, making a payment, signing, or deleting, it shows exactly what it will do and waits for approval; moving money and trading securities are off-limits entirely.
  • 6,000+ connectors — Gmail, Slack, QuickBooks, Stripe, Gusto, HubSpot, Docusign and Shopify among them, via the Claude Marketplace; included in Pro and Max at the same usage limits.
So what for you

If you juggle a lot of tools, Duet is worth the waitlist — but treat it like a capable assistant on probation: connect one or two apps first, keep the approval gate on, and watch what it flags before you widen its reach.

What it is

An agent that works ahead of you

Duet shifts Claude from a chat you open to an agent that comes to you. It carries over your memory, skills, and connected apps from your Claude account, keeps one ongoing thread you can type or talk to, and runs in the background on its own virtual machine with a browser — so a task keeps going after you close your laptop. When something is done or needs a decision, it notifies you.

We built Claude Duet for professionals and small business owners with more on their plates than they can track.

Why it matters

Proactive beats reactive

The value isn't a faster chatbot — it's catching the things you didn't think to ask about. Early testers describe it flagging a showing booked on the wrong day, an unpaid invoice, or a calendar event sitting right before a flight. The bet is that a standing agent that triages your day quietly is worth more than one that waits to be prompted.

Credentials

Never sees your secrets

Credential handling follows the Claude Managed Agents design, so Duet never directly sees your passwords or sign-in tokens.

Defenses

Built on the agent framework

Actions are checked by the auto-mode classifier; defenses against hidden instructions and prompt injection come from browser-use research. Red teams and an external firm pen-tested it.

Data tiers

Guards sensitive info

A dedicated classifier rejects sharing the strictest tier — including card numbers and government IDs. Health or finances pass only when you explicitly instruct it.

What it can do today

Tasks, purchases, and the rough edges

Tasks

Real work, not just replies

Schedule meetings, build dashboards with dozens of tools, run financial or sales forecasts, prototype, and build web apps. Larger jobs use more model turns and background work, which count toward your limits.

Purchases

Pays with a virtual card

Checkout uses Link (Stripe's wallet) or Shop Pay: Duet confirms the item, Link asks for spend approval, and it pays with a virtual card so your real number isn't shared.

Limits

Still a preview

It might misread a thread or miscalibrate urgency, personal errands like booking tickets are weaker, it can't yet show you its browser, and it lives in its own app, separate from the main Claude app.

Join the waitlist

Claude Duet is a research preview on iOS, Android, and claude.ai, starting with US Pro and Max users. It's included in those plans at the same usage limits as the rest of Claude — so the cost is your attention, spent on connecting apps carefully.
