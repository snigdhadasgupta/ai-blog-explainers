Box Puts Claude Fable 5.1 to Work

Box tests frontier models the way its customers actually use them — complex, multi-document business tasks run through an agent. On that benchmark, Claude Fable 5.1 scored 72% to Fable 5’s 65%, and did it 23% faster using 25% fewer tokens.

The idea in five lines

  • Real work, not trivia — Box grades models on complex, multi-document business tasks run through an agent that must find files, reason across them, and hand off a finished deliverable.
  • A seven-point jump — Claude Fable 5.1 scored 72% overall versus Fable 5’s 65%, with the biggest gains in analytical work where one early error cascades.
  • Faster and leaner too — average task-completion time fell 23% and total token usage fell 25%, so accuracy didn’t come at the cost of speed or spend.
  • Analytical tasks led — data analysis rose 56% to 69%, report drafting 70% to 76%, and expert review and verification 60% to 66%.
  • Industry swings were large — financial services 63% to 82%, public sector 58% to 74%, and technology 36% to 74%.
So what for you

If your team has kept AI to “ask a question about one document,’ this eval is the signal that end-to-end analytical workflows — forecasts, report drafting, cross-file verification — are now reliable enough to pilot. The higher accuracy mainly buys you less manual re-checking before you act on the output.

Why the test is different

Graded on finished work, not one prompt

Enterprise work rarely fits in a single document or a single prompt. Box’s evaluation makes an agent reconcile information across spreadsheets, PDFs, presentations, and images, perform multi-step analysis, apply domain-specific logic, and produce a deliverable a knowledge worker would actually hand off — then judges whether the finished work is correct and how efficiently it got there.

Fable 5.1’s biggest gains appear in analytical work — the tasks where one incorrect assumption cascades through every conclusion that follows.

That framing matters: in a long reasoning chain, an early mistake distorts every downstream number. Holding accuracy across the whole chain is what separates a model that can be trusted with an end-to-end workflow from one that just answers questions.
