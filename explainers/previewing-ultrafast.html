How OpenAI uses it

Tightening the loop from signal to action

Inside OpenAI, developers use Ultrafast for incident response — reading logs, analyzing traces, synthesizing conversations, and helping prepare or validate a fix in a fraction of the time, with Sol’s intelligence, while engineers keep judgment and deployment. For research, a common pattern of launching a batch of experiments overnight and reviewing in the morning tightens into multiple iterations during the workday.
