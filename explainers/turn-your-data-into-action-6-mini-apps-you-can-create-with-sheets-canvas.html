Turn a Spreadsheet Into a Mini App

Sheets canvas uses Gemini 3.8 Flash to turn spreadsheet rows into interactive mini apps from a plain-language prompt — Kanban boards, executive dashboards, scenario models — where visual edits update the underlying data and everything stays inside the sheet.

The idea in five lines

  • Rows become apps — describe what you want and Sheets canvas turns your data into an interactive view — no code, no third-party tool.
  • Edits sync both ways — changes made in the visual app update the underlying spreadsheet automatically.
  • Six ready patterns — Kanban boards, executive dashboards, financial scenario models, event schedules, 2×2 priority matrices, and workshop boards.
  • Shares like a file — everything lives in the sheet, so it shares through the file permissions you already use.
  • Fast model under it — Gemini 3.8 Flash powers generation, on Business/Enterprise Standard and Plus, AI Pro/Ultra, and Education plans.
So what for you

If your team keeps rebuilding the same tracker or dashboard by hand, Sheets canvas collapses that into a prompt on data you already maintain. Start with the view you recreate most often — a status board or a KPI dashboard — and let the sheet stay the single source of truth instead of exporting to a separate tool.

The idea

The data was already there — now it’s usable

A spreadsheet holds the data but rarely the right view of it, so teams export to separate tools or rebuild boards by hand. Sheets canvas generates an interactive app directly from the rows: describe the view you need, and changes made visually flow straight back into the underlying data.

Everything lives within your sheet — so it shares through standard file permissions, and stays synchronized in real time across the team.

Why it stays trustworthy

One source of truth, not a copy

Because the app is generated on top of the sheet rather than exported from it, there’s no second copy to drift out of date. Visual edits write back to the data, sharing uses the same permissions as any Google Sheet, and the whole thing stays synchronized across everyone who has access.

Replace your most-rebuilt view

Name the dashboard or board your team recreates every week, prompt Sheets canvas to build it on the live data, and share it with normal file permissions — the sheet stays the source of truth, and the manual rebuild goes away.
