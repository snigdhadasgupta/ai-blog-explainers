Workspace Drops: Image AI, Interactive Data, Cross-App Help

The September drop brings pro-level AI image editing (Google Pics), one-click interactive mini-apps in Sheets, cross-app task automation, a unified Ask Gemini in Chat, document-to-video in Vids, and Gemini-based data classification in Drive.

The idea in five lines

  • Pro-level image editing — Google Pics (built on Nano Banana) does object-level edits, text changes, cropping, and 2K/4K upscaling inside Docs and Slides.
  • Sheets become mini-apps — one click turns spreadsheet data into Kanban boards, planners, and dashboards, synced in real time, no code.
  • Work across apps without switching — Workspace Intelligence generates Docs, Sheets, and Slides in the background and drafts email from inside other apps.
  • One place to Ask Gemini — a unified Chat interface spans Gmail, Calendar, Drive, and conversations to search, summarize, and schedule.
  • Docs to video, data auto-classified — Vids turns Docs and PDFs into explainer videos; Gemini classifies Drive files for DLP and retention at scale.
So what for you

If your teams live in Workspace, the theme is less context-switching: Sheets-as-apps and cross-app automation collapse steps people do by hand. Pick one recurring report or board and rebuild it with Sheets canvas to see the payoff fast.

Under the hood

What’s new vs. nice-to-have

Google Pics is built on the Nano Banana model, with batch edits and easy reversion. Sheets canvas builds layouts from natural-language prompts with two-way sync to the source sheet, so the app and the data never drift. And Gemini-based classification improves DLP accuracy by labeling files at scale from plain-language instructions.

What to do next

Don’t try to adopt all six at once. Pick the one that maps to a weekly chore — a status dashboard (Sheets canvas), a recurring explainer (Vids), or file labeling for DLP (Drive classification) — and standardize it for the team.
