Automating the Workday with Workspace Studio

Google's Executive Productivity Advisor walks through how she offloads the busywork of an overloaded workday to Workspace Studio — a no-code command center where plain-English descriptions become background flows that move work across Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Meet, and Chat without a prompt each time.

The idea in five lines

  • Workspace Studio is a command center for no-code, AI-automated flows — "task specialists" that work in the background around the clock instead of waiting for you to prompt Gemini.
  • Flows span multiple apps: the real strength is multi-step work that moves data between Gmail, Docs, and Sheets — detect an invoice in email, extract the data, log it in a Sheet for analysis.
  • Triggers are scheduled or event-based: a daily morning briefing runs on a clock; a follow-up draft fires when a meeting ends; a commitment reminder fires off a phrase in a Meet transcript.
  • It pairs with Gemini "skills" that add specialized instructions, so a flow can carry your team's know-how, not just generic steps.
  • No coding required — if you can describe the workflow in plain English, you can build it at studio.workspace.google.com.
So what for you

Pick the one recurring chore that eats your mornings — triaging mail, rebuilding a to-do list, chasing status — and describe it as a flow in one plain-English sentence. Start with a scheduled briefing or an auto-built task list; those are low-risk, high-relief, and they teach you how triggers behave before you automate anything that sends on your behalf.

What it is

A "no-code command center" for Gemini

The piece is framed as an advice column: an account manager named Gwen, buried in work, asks whether Gemini can take tasks off her plate. The answer is Workspace Studio — where you build your own automations, described as task specialists that run in the background 24/7. It connects Workspace apps so they can act without you prompting Gemini every time, and its main strength is multi-step flows that span several apps.

If you can describe a workflow in plain English, you can build it — no coding required.

A worked example: rather than asking Gemini to find an invoice in your email, a flow detects invoices on its own, extracts the data, and logs it in a Sheet ready for analysis. Flows can also draw on Gemini "skills" that layer in specialized instructions.

Getting started

Open Workspace Studio, describe one background flow in plain English, and let it run before you add more. Begin with read-and-summarize flows (briefings, to-do lists, status scans) that save time with little risk, then graduate to flows that draft follow-ups or act across apps once you trust how the triggers fire.
