ChatGPT Ads Hits $1B — and Goes Global

In under 200 days, ChatGPT Ads reached a $1 billion annualized run rate with tens of thousands of advertisers. OpenAI is now opening self-service buying across India, Europe, the Middle East, and North Africa — funding a free tier for more than a billion weekly users.

The idea in five lines

  • $1B run rate in <200 days — ChatGPT Ads is now a pillar of OpenAI’s business alongside subscriptions, enterprise, and APIs.
  • Ads fund free access — an ad-supported tier helps keep ChatGPT available to more than a billion weekly active users.
  • Context-based and labeled — ads use the conversation’s context, stay separate from answers, and don’t change what ChatGPT says.
  • Now self-service — Ads Manager opens across India, Europe, the Middle East, and North Africa; 40+ countries, 50+ partners.
  • Real outcomes cited — one advertiser reported 3× ROAS over 28 days; a partner said 80%+ of ad-driven traffic was new customers.
So what for you

If you own product or marketing, ChatGPT is becoming a discovery-and-decision surface, not just an answer box — worth watching how consideration-stage traffic behaves there, and noting that placements are context-based and kept separate from the model’s answers.

The shift

ChatGPT as a decision surface

People arrive at ChatGPT with concrete goals — choosing business software, planning a renovation, looking for a job, learning a language. They lay out what they want, what matters, and their constraints, and ChatGPT helps them frame the decision, set criteria, weigh alternatives, and move toward action. Discovery, consideration, and decision now happen in one place.

ChatGPT Ads inserts marketers into that flow: the system uses the context of the conversation (and, where enabled by country and settings, a user’s broader ChatGPT context) to show a relevant ad. OpenAI frames advertising as one pillar of a diversified model alongside subscriptions, enterprise, and usage-based APIs — with an ad-supported free tier that helps keep ChatGPT available to more than a billion weekly users.

The strategic read: ChatGPT is becoming a place where purchase decisions take shape, not just an answer box.

Guardrails

Built to protect trust

Labeling

Clearly marked, kept separate

Ads are always labeled and separated from ChatGPT’s answers — a visible boundary between editorial response and paid placement.

Independence

Answers don’t change

Advertising does not influence the answers ChatGPT provides. The model’s response is meant to stand on its own regardless of who is buying ads.

Privacy

No access to conversations

Advertisers do not receive access to people’s private conversations; targeting works off context, not a handoff of chat content.

Control

Users tune personalization

People can control how their ad experience is personalized, and OpenAI says trust and relevance metrics have held through the rollout.

Under the hood

From managed pilot to self-serve platform

ChatGPT Ads began as a managed-sales, agency, and partner-led model. The introduction of Ads Manager in May opened it to small and medium-sized businesses, which now make up a material share of the base. Starting this rollout, self-service buying expands across India, Europe, the Middle East, and North Africa — on top of availability in 40-plus countries.

The buying stack has matured too. CPC and outcome-optimized bidding now account for the majority of campaigns, while a Pixel and Conversions API have become the foundations for measurement and optimization. Product feeds, platform and geographic targeting, and custom audiences let advertisers narrow to the customers most relevant to them — the familiar toolkit of a modern ad platform, now attached to a conversational surface.

What to watch

OpenAI says the next phase brings more markets plus new formats, objectives, buying options, and measurement — and “more native” ways for businesses to interact with people in ChatGPT. For product and marketing teams, the thing to track is how “consideration” traffic behaves on a surface where the answer and the ad sit side by side but separate.
