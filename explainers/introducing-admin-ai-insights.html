The problem

Between tokenmaxxing and kneejerk cuts

In the past few quarters, plenty of companies went all-in on AI only to discover they’d blown past their projected usage budgets and had to recalibrate. The technology isn’t going anywhere — the upside for speed and efficiency is too large to ignore — but to integrate AI fully, companies need deep insight into how they consume it and which parts of the org benefit, so future budgets can be forecast with confidence.

This isn’t about maximizing token consumption or cutting it off completely. To keep ahead, organizations need to employ every token that generates value without wasting resources where it doesn’t. We need token optimization.

Insight enables optimization

A typical Admin AI Insights workflow

1

Pilot small

Run Box AI with one small group for a quarter and watch the dashboard to establish a baseline.

2

Investigate spikes

A usage spike from the Product team turns out to be token-heavy prototyping — move it to a more efficient model with light guidelines.

3

Fund what works

A marketing surge is a new workflow that massively improves output — the org adjusts budget and rolls it out wider.

By studying token usage, the organization grows its AI deployment while concentrating on the areas that drive the highest impact and trimming waste — optimization, not restriction.

Two payoffs · What’s next

Budgeting, transparency, and audit trails

Payoff 1

Better budgeting and forecasting

Visibility into which users and functions consume AI Units — and why — keeps usage aligned with the monthly budget and helps prepare for new deployments using historic patterns.

Payoff 2

Increased usage transparency

Deep insight into where AI is used and by whom helps compliance-minded admins spot potential issues sooner and regulate usage more precisely.

Next on the roadmap: AI audit-trail tools that let companies audit and report on how agents access or create enterprise content — tracking where and how agents touch content as an equal partner to managing token consumption.

Make AI predictable and profitable

Admin AI Insights turns AI from an unpredictable line item into something you can plan around — monitor consumption, redirect waste toward value, and forecast the next deployment before you commit the budget. It’s available now to organizations using Box AI.
