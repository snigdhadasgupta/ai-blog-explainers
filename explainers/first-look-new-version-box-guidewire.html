A First Look at the New Box for Guidewire

Unstructured documents are the bulk of insurance evidence — and the hardest thing to operationalize. The new Box for Guidewire brings AI document intake and data extraction into claims and underwriting, inside the system of record.

The idea in five lines

  • Documents stay in Box, work stays in Guidewire — adjusters and underwriters access, preview, and manage Box-hosted documents and metadata without leaving the Guidewire UI.
  • AI does the intake — with Box AI and Box Extract configured, incoming evidence is auto-classified and key data extracted for triage, policy processing, and claims review.
  • Outsiders submit safely — claimants, brokers, and repair shops send documents through controlled Box intake paths with no direct Guidewire access, under full governance.
  • Less custom glue — the new version adds folder and user mapping, authentication, embedded preview, and metadata sync, cutting the custom integration work insurers used to carry.
  • Humans still decide — extracted data is routed to adjusters and underwriters, who remain responsible for claim and risk decisions; source documents stay governed in Box.
So what for you

If your team runs a core system of record (insurance or otherwise) alongside a separate document store, this is the pattern to copy: let the system of record own the process and the content platform own the files — connected by AI extraction — instead of maintaining brittle custom connectors or re-keying data by hand.

The problem

Adjusters live in Guidewire; the evidence lives everywhere else

Claims adjusters and underwriters work inside Guidewire, but the supporting documents arrive by email, portals, and uploads: medical records, police reports, estimates, loss runs, broker submissions. Teams read them and then manually key the data Guidewire needs — and many insurers maintain custom connections between Guidewire and a document repository, which adds implementation and ongoing maintenance work.

Box for Guidewire is already in the Guidewire Marketplace across all three core products. Guidewire stays the system of record for claims, policy, and billing; Box manages the documents tied to those records — intake, AI-assisted review, approvals, and retention.

What changed

The new version removes custom work

Built by Guidewire with Box, the new version is designed to reduce the custom build and maintenance an implementation used to need. It extends the connection so users work with Box-managed documents and metadata through the Guidewire interface — uploading, updating, and deleting documents without leaving it.

Inside Guidewire

Preview + manage in place

Cover folder and user mapping, authentication, embedded content preview, and metadata sync mean an adjuster never has to context-switch to find a document.

Governed intake

External parties, no system access

Claimants, brokers, repair shops, medical providers, and counsel submit through Box intake paths; classification, access controls, retention, legal holds, and audit history apply per the carrier’s configuration.

The split is deliberate: Guidewire remains the system of record while Box carries the document lifecycle around it. Because external parties submit into Box rather than into Guidewire, carriers keep a clean governance boundary — classification, retention, legal holds, and audit trails on every inbound file — without widening access to their core platform.
