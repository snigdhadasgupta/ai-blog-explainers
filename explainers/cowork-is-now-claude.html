One Claude, No Modes

Claude Cowork and chat are becoming one Claude. You stop choosing a mode before you start — Claude figures out what a task needs — and Cowork and Design capabilities are available from any conversation, with new Docs and Slides built in.

The idea in five lines

  • No more modes — you hand over the task and Claude decides what it needs; Cowork and Design work from any conversation. ‘You keep the final say.’
  • Docs and Slides, built in — co-write documents and have Claude draft editable decks you can present or download as PowerPoint or PDF.
  • Everything carries over — existing chats, projects, artifacts, connectors and skills come with you.
  • Work that keeps going — Claude can keep working after you close your laptop, and you can check progress from your phone.
  • You set the oversight — Claude asks before acting by default, but you can let it run and interrupt only when something needs review.
So what for you

The practical change is that you stop choosing a tool and start handing over outcomes. Pick one recurring deliverable — a weekly report, a standing deck — and delegate the whole thing, including a schedule, then set your check-in level so routine work runs with less interruption and sensitive actions still pause for approval.

Why it matters

Delegation, not mode-switching

The old model made you decide up front whether a request belonged in chat, Cowork or Design. Merging them removes that choice: you describe the outcome and Claude assembles what it needs. For recurring work — a report and the slides that go with it — keeping everything in one conversation means the outputs stay aligned.

What to do next

Try delegating a whole task rather than deciding where it belongs — a recurring report or deck — and schedule it to run on its own. Keep the default approval step for sensitive actions, and dial down check-ins for routine work.
