Choctaw Nation, Secured and Automated

The third-largest Indian Nation in the US runs nearly 500 services for 225,000+ members — much of it on the most sensitive data there is. Its move: one secure, AI-enabled content foundation, with no-code apps replacing paper and email and Box AI layered on top.

The idea in five lines

  • Security came first, not AI — a sovereign government handling W-9s, child-welfare records, and domestic-violence case files needed to stop collecting sensitive data by email before anything else.
  • No-code apps replaced paper: juror check-in, a public-defender evidence repository, and an employee-loan program were rebuilt on Box Forms, Automate, Sign, Extract, and Doc Gen — 23 custom apps in all.
  • The outcomes are operational: the Nation reports saving more than 360 hours a year, juror payments going from two weeks to immediate, and loan turnaround dropping from a 30-day minimum to a single pay period.
  • AI sits on the foundation: public defenders use Box AI to sift discovery bundles and transcripts, and the treasury team uses AI-powered search across 15 years of migrated investment and legal records.
  • Sovereignty is the frame: a past data breach cost more than the entire first Box rollout, so secure, governed content is treated as a condition of the Nation’s compacts and standing — not an IT nicety.
So what for you

If you’re weighing where AI fits in a regulated or public-sector operation, note the order here: get content secure and governed first, automate the paper processes, then layer AI on the clean foundation. The time savings came from automation; AI became safely useful because the access controls and single source of truth were already in place.

Why it matters

When the data is this sensitive, the breach cost sets the budget

The Choctaw Nation of Oklahoma serves more than 225,000 tribal members across a 10,923-square-mile reservation, running nearly 500 separate services — a housing authority, an education foundation, a health services authority, and justice and public-safety offices among them. Many of those services run on exactly the data you least want leaking: tax forms, child-case records, and domestic-violence survivor files.

Caseloads surged after a US Supreme Court ruling expanded tribal jurisdiction, and a past data breach ended up costing more than the Nation’s entire first Box implementation. That math reframed secure, governed content from a cost center into a prerequisite — and it is why the biggest driver of the project was visibility and control over access, not AI.

“We had to find a way to collect sensitive data a lot more securely than by email.” — Todd Hughes, Senior Director for IT, Justice & Travel Services

What they built

Three flagship workflows on one platform

Juror check-in

From form to paid

An online fillable form auto-generates the juror oath and a W-9, captures an e-signature, and custom code even calculates round-trip mileage from the home address — cutting hours of daily check-in work.

Public defenders

Evidence in one place

Case documents moved off local storage into a governed Box repository after evidence turned up in five different places — with no-code apps built on Box Forms, Box AI, and Box Automate.

Employee loans

Paper to pay period

A paper loan process was digitized with Box Apps, Automate, and Sign, wired to credit, HR, and payroll — dropping turnaround from a 30-day minimum to one pay period.

Where AI actually fits

A clean foundation first, then Box AI on top

The Nation’s leaders are explicit that the foundation did the heavy lifting: secure intake, consistent permissions, and a single source of truth. What that foundation unlocked is an “AI-enabled content foundation” — AI applied to governed content rather than a scattered mess.

Box AI

Discovery review

Public defenders use Box AI to sift large discovery bundles and transcripts, improving response times and case-prep efficiency against a sharply higher caseload.

AI-powered search

15 years of records

The finance and treasury team searches roughly 15 years of migrated investment, audit, and legal documents with AI-powered search instead of hunting through folders.

Box Agent

Right model per task

Box Agent lets teams apply different models to different tasks — keeping the AI layer flexible as needs and models change, without re-plumbing the content.

The sequencing is the lesson. Because access was already scoped and content already consolidated, AI could be pointed at sensitive justice and finance records without widening exposure — the opposite of bolting a model onto an ungoverned file share.

The takeaway for other enterprises

Lead with secure intake and governed access, let your own teams build the no-code apps that replace paper, and measure the win in reclaimed hours and faster turnaround. Treat AI as the layer you add once the content is clean and the permissions are tight — especially when the data carries legal, financial, or public-trust weight.
