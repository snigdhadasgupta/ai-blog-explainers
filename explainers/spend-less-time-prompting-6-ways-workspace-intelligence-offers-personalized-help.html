Workspace Intelligence: less prompting, more done

Google’s Workspace Intelligence layer builds a real-time, unified picture of your work across apps — so Gemini can help without elaborate prompts or setup. Here are the six places it shows up, and how it stays inside your existing permissions.

The idea in five lines

  • Context without prompting — Workspace Intelligence reads across your apps to understand your projects, relationships and work style, with no setup required.
  • Chat and Drive catch you up — daily briefings and a searchable team knowledge base in Chat; AI Overviews and grouped Drive projects that answer instead of making you hunt.
  • Gmail and Docs sound like you — one-shot drafts in your voice using team and project context; Docs that assemble citable first drafts from your recent files.
  • Slides and Sheets stay on-brand and live — decks that match your templates and fonts; spreadsheets built in natural language with real, editable formulas.
  • Permission-aware by design — Gemini respects existing file permissions, content isn’t used for ads or to train models without permission, and admins control which services feed it.
So what for you

If your org runs on Workspace, the payoff is less prompt-crafting — the assistant already carries cross-app context — so point people at the in-app entry points (Help me write, Ask Gemini, Sheets natural language) and confirm your admin data-sharing controls match your policy.

What it is

A context layer under the apps

Workspace Intelligence isn’t a new app — it’s a layer that gives Gemini a unified, real-time understanding of your work so you don’t have to re-explain yourself every time. It rests on four ideas: it gathers information across apps for full project context, keeps situational awareness of your relationships and active projects, personalizes to your role and work style, and requires no setup to start.

Gathers

Information across apps

Pulls the relevant files, chats, and mail into context so answers reflect the whole project, not one document.

Knows

Situational awareness

Tracks your work relationships and which projects are active, so help is timely and relevant.

Personalizes

To your role and style

Learns from how you work — your voice, your team, your patterns — rather than treating every user the same.

Just works

No setup required

Operates immediately, with no configuration or prompt scaffolding to build first.

Where it shows up

Six apps, six kinds of help

Chat

Stay on top of projects

Daily briefings of key updates, questions answered in-thread, and automatic search of your team’s secure knowledge base.

Drive

Answers, not file hunts

AI Overviews scan files for quick answers; Ask Gemini turns Drive into a living knowledge base and groups related docs into projects.

Gmail

Drafts that sound like you

One-shot drafts in your voice, aware of team hierarchy and project history; an AI inbox that surfaces urgent action items.

Docs

Blank page to first draft

Personalized drafts that connect details from recent files, chats, emails and notes into a complete, citable draft.

Slides

On-brand decks

Checks existing team decks and brand templates, then generates editable slides that match your fonts and style.

Sheets

Scattered data, organized

Builds live, fully editable tables with native formulas from natural language and cross-app context.

Security & control

Personalized, but inside your permissions

Permissions

Respected, not bypassed

Gemini’s access honors existing file permissions — it only sees what you can already see.

Your data

Not sold, not ad-fuel

Content isn’t sold, used for ads, or used to train models without permission.

Admin control

Which services feed it

Administrators decide which Workspace services supply data to Gemini, with client-side encryption available for sensitive content.

Getting value from it

Point teams at the in-app entry points — Help me write in Gmail, Ask Gemini in Drive and Chat, natural-language builds in Sheets — and confirm your admin controls over which services feed Gemini match your data policy before a broad rollout.
