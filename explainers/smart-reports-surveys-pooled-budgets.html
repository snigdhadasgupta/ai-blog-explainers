See the Work, Fund the Team

Three Claude Enterprise features shipped today: smart reports that show what each team does with Claude and what it costs, in-app surveys that capture where the value lands, and pooled group budgets that fund a team instead of a person.

The idea in five lines

  • The real problem — some teams get huge value from Claude and others stall, and admins had no clean way to see which is which or why.
  • Smart reports analyze a sample of a team’s transcripts and group the work into workstreams, each with its spend, deliverables, and most common friction points.
  • In-app surveys ask members, right after a reply, what was most valuable and what would break first if Claude went away — responses stream to a page admins can export.
  • Pooled group budgets give a team one shared monthly budget, so heavy users stop hitting personal limits while others leave budget unspent.
  • Explicitly not for ranking people — reports are for adoption and investment planning, not evaluating individuals or making employment decisions.
So what for you

If your org already pays for Claude Enterprise, run a smart report on one team that’s clearly getting value and look at its top workstreams and friction points — that is the fastest way to find the workflow worth turning into a shared skill for everyone else.

Why it matters

Take what already works and spread it

In most organizations, a handful of teams quietly figure out how to make Claude indispensable while the rest never get past experimenting. The gap is rarely the model — it is visibility. Admins couldn’t see what the winning teams actually did, what it cost, or where everyone else got stuck. These three features turn that invisible usage into something you can read, measure, and fund.

The job isn’t to monitor people — it’s to find the workflow that works on one team and make it the default for every team.

What shipped

Three features, one loop

Measure

Smart reports

Pick a team, date range, and products. Claude groups the work into workstreams and shows spend, deliverables, and friction — a missing connector, a failed tool call — plus one consolidated list of recommendations you can share as an artifact.

Listen

In-app surveys

Default questions focus on value: the most valuable thing done recently, how much time it saved, and what would break first without Claude. Members answer on a short card inside the tool they’re already using.

Fund

Pooled budgets

A group shares one monthly budget while member limits still guard against runaway individual use. When someone hits a limit they request more from inside Claude, and admins review.

Governance is built in. Surveys can be enabled for organizations using customer-managed encryption keys, and for a single-retention-period org the responses delete on that same schedule. Owners can also let non-admin team leads run reports for their own groups, so the people closest to the work can read it.

Admins with billing permission get email alerts at each threshold of a budget; setup suggests an amount from recent usage.

Where to start

All three are generally available today for Claude Enterprise. Run one smart report, send one survey to a team already seeing value, and set a pooled budget for a group that keeps bumping individual limits — then turn the top recommendation into a skill the whole org inherits.
