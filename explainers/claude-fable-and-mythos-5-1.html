Claude Fable 5.1 & Mythos 5.1

Anthropic’s most advanced models for coding and knowledge work land with a rare combination: higher scores, a ~25% lower bill, zero-data-retention-grade privacy for enterprises, and early evidence that a frontier model can make real scientific discoveries.

The idea in five lines

  • Same model, two safeguard levels — Fable 5.1 is generally available; Mythos 5.1 is the identical model with looser safeguards, offered only to vetted cyber and life-sciences teams.
  • Roughly 25% cheaper for typical workloads (up to ~45% for heavy agentic work), driven by a 75% cut to cache-read pricing — now $0.25 per million tokens.
  • Privacy without the tradeoff — new Enterprise Frontier Safeguards give the privacy of zero data retention while still catching misuse, with data held in the customer’s own cloud.
  • A real capability jump — state-of-the-art on agentic coding, science, and knowledge-work benchmarks, and it found a one-in-a-million crash that stumped a top trading firm for years.
  • Science, not just demos — it designed protein binders 10× stronger than a competition’s best, remapped a third of Venus, and sped up biology models up to 2.5×.
So what for you

If your team already uses Fable or Opus, this is a low-risk upgrade: you likely get better output at a lower price by switching, and can dial “effort” down to match Fable 5 quality for even less. If you’re in a regulated industry, the paired Enterprise Frontier Safeguards are the reason to start the procurement conversation now.

What actually shipped

A better model that also costs less

Claude Fable 5.1 sets a new bar for coding, knowledge work, and long-running agentic tasks — and at Low or Medium “effort” it matches or beats last generation’s Fable 5 at much lower cost. In practice that means two levers: reach for High effort when you want the ceiling, or drop the effort dial to hold quality steady and spend less. (Fable 5.1 defaults to High in Claude Code, Medium in Cowork and on Claude.ai.)

Fable-level intelligence, Opus-level price, Sonnet-level speed — and it stays readable over long, multi-step tasks.

The price change is structural, not a promotion. Cache reads — where the model re-uses context it has already processed — now cost 75% less. Because agentic work is context-heavy, that discount compounds: about 25% cheaper for a typical workload and up to ~45% for highly agentic ones. All other pricing is unchanged at $10 per million input and $50 per million output tokens.

Why enterprises care

Privacy and safeguards, rebalanced

Data retention

ZDR-grade privacy

New Enterprise Frontier Safeguards store activity data in cloud infrastructure the customer controls — the privacy of zero data retention, without giving up misuse detection. Rolling out in phases from this fall; eligible customers get ZDR on Fable in the meantime.

Cyber

60% fewer false alarms

Updated cyber safeguards fire around 60% less often per Claude Code session, and Fable 5.1 can now be used to find software vulnerabilities — defensive work — though exploit generation still routes to Opus models.

Biology

85% fewer benign blocks

Biology safeguards fire 85% less often on elementary and medical questions. Genuine life-sciences R&D is served through a vetted Mythos 5.1 access program built with the US government.

The qualitative signal matched the scores. Investment firm Millennium reported that Fable 5.1 found the cause of an extremely rare crash — roughly one in a million runs — that its engineers and every other model had failed to explain for four to five years, by disassembling a vendor library and tracing it to a bug there.

Beyond software

Early, concrete scientific results

Molecular design

10× stronger binders

Given open-source protein tools, Mythos 5.1 designed binders with affinities 10× higher than the best entries in Adaptyv Bio’s competitions, and hit ~50% viable designs across 12 targets vs the 10–15% typical today.

Planetary science

Remapped Venus

Fable 5.1 trained a network on 30-year-old Magellan radar to build a new elevation map of a third of Venus — resolving 2–3 km detail instead of 10–20 km, released under Creative Commons ahead of NASA and ESA missions.

Comp bio

2.5× faster models

By writing custom GPU kernels, Mythos 5.1 sped up seven open-source biology models up to 2.5× with identical outputs — cutting genome-wide GPU costs 30–60%, work that normally takes performance engineers weeks.

On safety, Anthropic reports Mythos 5.1 is better aligned than its predecessor across most metrics — less likely to reach outside its test environment, use motivated reasoning, or reward-hack — while noting it can still sometimes bypass approvals, and that coverage of long-context and multi-agent settings remains limited. Full detail is in the system card.

What to do about it

Fable 5.1 is available today on the Claude API (claude-fable-5-1), AWS, Google Cloud, and Azure. If you run Fable or Opus workloads, benchmark a switch at Medium effort to bank the cost savings; if you’re regulated, register interest in Enterprise Frontier Safeguards before the fall rollout.
