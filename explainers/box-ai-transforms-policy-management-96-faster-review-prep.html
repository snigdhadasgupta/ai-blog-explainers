96% Faster Policy Review Prep at Box

Box’s internal Policies & Standards team replaced hours of PDF scrolling with a metadata-extraction template and a Box Apps dashboard — cutting quarterly review prep 96% and saving roughly $14,000 in year one.

The idea in five lines

  • Nearly 100 policies — once needed manual PDF scrolling every quarter to pull owners, sign-off dates, and approvers.
  • A metadata template — extracts the key fields straight from each PDF into a searchable Box Apps dashboard.
  • Prep dropped 96% — a quarter’s setup fell from about 12 hours to roughly 2, saving an estimated $14,000 in year one.
  • A self-service Box Hub — lets employees find and ask policies directly — over 6,200 views since launch.
  • Box Automate is next — triggering review tickets and assigning owners from the same metadata, with people still in control.
So what for you

For any recurring compliance task buried in documents, the reframe that pays off is “how do we make our content answer this itself?” Extract structured metadata once and let a dashboard stay current instead of re-typing spreadsheets each quarter.

The invisible quarterly grind

Work that’s costly only when it goes wrong

Every quarter, Box’s Policies & Standards team manually opened each of nearly 100 policies, scrolled to signature pages, and pulled the details that mattered: who owned it, when it was last signed, and whether it was due for review. Ownership data lived across PDFs, Box Notes, spreadsheets, and someone’s memory — there was no single source of truth.

The stakes were real: a missed review could jeopardize a certification renewal or shake a customer’s confidence that Box meets its compliance obligations. So the team built a metadata template that turns every policy PDF into structured, searchable data feeding a reliable inventory dashboard.

Then & now

One reframe changed the whole job

Before Manual tracking

Excel plus the policy PDFs themselves — scroll each document, extract the last-update date, owner, and approver names by hand. By the time it was done, it was already out of date.

After A single source of truth

A metadata template pulls the most accurate data straight from each PDF into a Box Apps dashboard that stays current in real time — the team’s most reliable policy inventory.

How the template works

From PDF to dashboard to self-service

The template tells the system exactly what to pull from each document, and where:

Extract

Fields, by location

Policy ID from the header, organization from the footer, policy name from the title page, change and reason from the revision-history table, and owner, approvers, and last signature date from the final approvals page.

Organize

Box Apps dashboard

Extracted metadata feeds a custom dashboard that sorts ~100 policies by quarter and by org — no manual sorting, and the data updates in real time.

Self-serve

Box Hub + AI answers

Employees search and read policies directly, and ask the AI agent a plain question — e.g., the policy on traveling with company equipment — getting an answer pulled straight from the relevant policy.

Next up, in pilot: a Box Automate workflow that triggers each review and assigns ownership automatically from the same metadata — turning error-prone busywork into a workflow the team can trust, with people still owning key decisions.

The lesson beyond the tooling

Instead of asking “how do we hire more people to keep up with this?” the team asked “how do we make our existing content answer these questions itself?” That reframe turned a recurring quarterly headache into a problem that largely solves itself — a template other teams can copy.
