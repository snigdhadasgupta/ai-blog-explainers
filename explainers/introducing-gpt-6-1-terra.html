GPT-6.1 Terra: From Vision to Action

GPT-6.1 Terra is a fast, efficient model that combines detailed visual reasoning with precise computer use and clearer writing — aimed squarely at the workday tasks of drafting, interpreting drawings, filling forms, and editing, at half the API price of GPT-5.6 Terra.

The idea in five lines

  • Vision plus action — Terra pairs detailed visual reasoning with precise computer use, for workday tasks like drafting proposals, reading technical drawings, filling forms, and editing videos.
  • Half the price — standard API pricing is 50% lower than GPT-5.6 Terra and GPT-6.1 Sol, which makes high-volume automation practical.
  • Big agentic gains — on Agents’ Last Exam it scores 15 points higher than GPT-5.6 Terra at about a third of the cost, and matches GPT-6 Astra at a tenth the price per token.
  • Fewer mistakes — on an internal error benchmark the low-effort error rate fell from 53.6% to 16.7%, and to 4.3% at max effort.
  • Available now — in ChatGPT Work and Codex for Plus, Pro, Enterprise, and Edu, and via the API as gpt-6.1-terra.
So what for you

If you’ve been pricing out agent automation on high-volume, screen-and-document work — reading invoices into accounting software, filling forms, logging video — Terra is the one to re-run your cost model against. The jump in computer-use quality at roughly a third of the cost is where the ROI changes.

Why it matters

The cost-intelligence frontier moves again

Terra’s pitch isn’t to be the smartest model — for the hardest coding and reasoning, OpenAI still points to GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6 Astra. Terra’s job is a new balance of speed, capability, and cost for the communication and computer-based work that fills an ordinary workday. At half the API price of the prior Terra, automating things like reading incoming invoices and entering their details into accounting software becomes practical at volume.

A step up from GPT-5.6 Terra on the tasks that fill the workday — and about half the price per token.
