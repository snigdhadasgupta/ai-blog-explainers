Albertsons Reimagines Retail with OpenAI

Across 2,200+ stores serving 36 million weekly customers, Albertsons is layering ChatGPT Enterprise for employees with customer-facing apps on the OpenAI API — a recipe-to-cart Safeway experience, explainable promotions, and faster merchandising.

The idea in five lines

  • Deployed across 2,200+ stores — Albertsons, Safeway, Vons, Jewel-Osco, Shaw’s, ACME, Tom Thumb — serving 36M weekly customers.
  • Recipe to cart in ChatGPT — a Safeway plugin turns a recipe, photo, or ‘plan pizza night for four’ into products, savings, and checkout.
  • Employees use ChatGPT Enterprise — digital, store ops, fulfillment, and merchandising teams move concepts to production faster.
  • Explainable recommendations — predictive models plus generative AI produce promo insights and merchant decision support, not black-box picks.
  • Pilot, then scale — focused pilots first, repeatable practices next, enterprise rollout last; the Safeway experience extends across banners.
So what for you

If you run a consumer business, Albertsons shows the two-sided play: ChatGPT Enterprise for internal speed and an API-built customer experience on top. Start where the data and demand already exist — shoppers show more openness to AI for groceries than most categories.

Customer side

From ‘plan pizza night’ to checkout

A new Safeway ChatGPT plugin lets shoppers input a recipe, a photo, or a meal request — ‘plan pizza night for four’ — and receive product recommendations with savings, a built cart, and checkout routing. The experience is planned to extend to Albertsons, Vons, Jewel-Osco, Shaw’s, ACME, and Tom Thumb.

This is another practical way we are using AI to reduce friction and make everyday shopping easier. — Jill Pavlovich, SVP Digital Customer Experience

The timing isn’t accidental: dunnhumby data from February 2026 shows shoppers are more open to AI for grocery purchases than for other categories.

What to take from it

For consumer-facing AI, pair an internal productivity layer (ChatGPT Enterprise) with a customer experience built on the API, and sequence it: pilot one banner or category, prove a repeatable pattern, then extend across brands. Lead where customer openness is already high.
