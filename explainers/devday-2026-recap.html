DevDay 2026, Unpacked

OpenAI’s DevDay 2026 ran wide: always-on ‘Dots’ agents, GPT-6.1 Sol, an Ultrafast speed tier, an Agents API with computer use, team collaboration inside ChatGPT, and an enterprise marketplace — all aimed at moving from chat to delegated, agentic work.

The idea in five lines

  • Dots agents — always-on agents that handle ongoing responsibilities on your behalf, on Pro and Business Premium.
  • Sol and Ultrafast — GPT-6.1 Sol brings near-Astra intelligence cheaply; Ultrafast adds up to 8x faster generation.
  • Agents API with computer use — multi-agent coordination, tool search and context compaction, with infrastructure run by OpenAI.
  • ChatGPT becomes collaborative — Space, Pages, Team Tasks and @ChatGPT in Slack and Teams turn it into a team workspace.
  • A real marketplace — 32 initial enterprise partners and Sign in with ChatGPT across 16 partners.
So what for you

The through-line is delegation. Pilot one recurring task as a Dots agent or Team Task — something scheduled or event-triggered that eats your team’s time — and judge it on whether it runs unattended and reliably, not on demo polish.

Why it matters

From a model you prompt to agents that work

DevDay 2026 was less a model launch than a platform statement: the center of gravity is moving from one-off chat to agents and teams that carry ongoing work. With 1.2 billion weekly ChatGPT users as the distribution surface, the collaboration and marketplace pieces matter as much as the raw models.

For builders, the headline is the Agents API with computer use and managed infrastructure; for enterprises, it’s security options and budget-flexible partner integrations.
