Four Role-Based Claude Certifications

Anthropic is expanding its Claude Certification Program to four proctored, identity-verified credentials — one for each role a customer needs to actually deploy AI. The largest firms are already certifying people by the tens of thousands.

The idea in five lines

  • Three new role-based certifications join the program, mapping to the four largest roles that put Claude into production.
  • Every exam is proctored and identity-verified via Pearson — validated capability, not course attendance. Badges are issued through Credly.
  • Momentum is real: 36,000+ consultants certified across 1,300+ organizations since March; 400,000+ trained this year via Anthropic Partner Academy.
  • Certified-practitioner counts feed a firm’s tier in the Claude Partner Network — Global Premier requires 1,000 certified, 100 customers across 3 regions, and 15 public endorsements.
  • Accenture (50k), PwC (30k), DXC & UST (20k each), Deloitte & KPMG (15k) and others have committed to certify at scale. Next tier review: October 1.
So what for you

If you're scaling AI use across a team, treat these role-based certifications as a concrete way to verify skills rather than assume them.

Why this matters

A credential buyers can actually trust

Anthropic’s stated goal is to be the leader of AI enablement. It launched the Claude Partner Network in March alongside a $100M investment, and added a Services Track in June to help enterprise buyers find the right firms. The missing piece: proof that a firm’s people are qualified — a benchmark customers, partners, and the industry recognize as verified expertise, not just a badge.

To make the credential mean something, certifications are granted through supervised, identity-verified exams that test the specific skills a role calls for — the same rigor that lets the credential be used to make real hiring and staffing decisions.

The line-up

The four credentials

Deploying Claude at a large enterprise involves different roles and specialties. The certifications map to the four biggest ones — every path starts at foundation level and advances to professional.

AssociateNew

Certified Associate: Foundations

Validates practical, everyday use of Claude for anyone on a Claude project — consultants, project leads, and both business and technical roles.

DeveloperNew

Certified Developer: Foundations

For engineers building applications with Claude — covering the Claude API, tool use, and agent development.

ArchitectNew

Certified Architect: Professional

The advanced credential: integration architecture, governance, and evaluation, built for the scale of large enterprises.

Architect

Certified Architect: Foundations

For solution architects who design and build agent systems with Claude.

Each certification was designed by subject-matter experts — including Anthropic’s own Applied AI team — so the content reflects the real work newly certified professionals do. Completed certifications are recognized with partner badges tied to a specific product area.

How it works

Proctored, verified, and decision-grade

Anthropic works with Pearson, using its global assessment and credentialing infrastructure. Here’s the path from prep to badge — and why the rigor matters.

1
Prepare

Take the prep course in Partner Academy

Certification preparation courses for each credential are available free in the Anthropic Partner Academy.

2
Verify

Validate your identity before the exam

Exams are delivered through Pearson Professional Assessments; test takers must confirm identity before beginning.

3
Sit

Take the proctored, supervised exam

Each exam is designed by subject-matter experts to reflect real field work, and administered under supervision.

4
Earn

Receive a digital badge via Credly

Passing earns a verifiable Credly by Pearson badge showing demonstrated expertise in a product area.

Why the ceremony? Because these credentials are used to make decisions. Certified-practitioner counts determine a firm’s standing in the Claude Partner Network and are weighed when companies vet who will work on their systems. The top tier, Global Premier, requires 1,000 certified practitioners, 100 customers across three regions, and 15 public customer endorsements — with the next tier review on October 1.

Get your people certified

Firms can join the Claude Partner Network and register practitioners for free. Prep courses live in the Anthropic Partner Academy.
